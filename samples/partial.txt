Cedar Lane Software Ltd. — Recruitment and Advancement Policy

Cedar Lane Software does not discriminate in hiring on the basis of race,
ancestry, place of origin, colour, ethnic origin, creed, sex, sexual
orientation, gender identity, age or disability. This includes First Nations,
Inuit and Métis applicants.

We want our workforce to reflect the communities we serve, including Indigenous
Peoples.

Indigenous candidates preferred for all client-facing roles.

Roles are advertised for at least two weeks. Interview panels have at least
two people and use the same questions for every candidate in a round.

New hires may ask their manager to suggest an informal mentor from another
team.

Promotions are considered once a year. Managers look at performance reviews
and time in role when recommending someone for promotion.

Questions about this policy can be sent to careers@cedarlane.example.

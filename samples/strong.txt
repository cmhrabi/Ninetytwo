Lakeshore Health Partners — Hiring, Retention and Advancement Policy

Lakeshore Health Partners prohibits discrimination and harassment in hiring,
pay, training and promotion on every ground in the Ontario Human Rights Code,
including race, ancestry, place of origin, colour, ethnic origin and creed. This
explicitly protects First Nations, Inuit and Métis applicants and employees.

Indigenous Hiring Program. We run a special program under section 14 of the
Ontario Human Rights Code. Its purpose is to address the under-representation
of Indigenous employees in clinical leadership roles at Lakeshore. The program
designates up to four Patient Navigator roles each year for Indigenous
applicants. It is reviewed every two years against workforce data and ends
when its goal is met.

Self-identification for the program is voluntary. Applicants may confirm their
identity through a letter from their community or an Indigenous organization of
their choice. This information is kept separately from the hiring file and used
only for the program.

Every new Indigenous hire is offered a formal sponsor from senior leadership for
their first two years. Sponsors meet with them monthly and advocate for
stretch assignments.

Promotion decisions use published, objective criteria for each level. Panels
score candidates against those criteria and record the reasons for each
decision. Records are kept for three years and reviewed each year for
disparities.

The Indigenous Employee Circle has an annual budget, paid meeting time for
members and an executive sponsor. The Circle is consulted when this policy is
reviewed.

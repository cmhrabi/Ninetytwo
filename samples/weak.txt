Northwind Freight Inc. — Hiring Policy

Northwind Freight is an equal opportunity employer. We hire the best person for
each role.

Hiring managers write the job description and post it on our careers page and on
job boards.

Candidates are screened by the hiring manager. Shortlisted candidates complete
one interview with the manager and one team member.

References are checked before an offer is made. Offers are approved by the
department head.

New staff complete a two-week onboarding program and a 90-day probation period.

Questions about this policy can be sent to the People team at
people@northwind.example or 416-555-0142.

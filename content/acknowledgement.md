We built NinetyTwo with the First Nations principles of OCAP® (Ownership, Control, Access and Possession) in mind. The tool uses no data about First Nations, Inuit or Métis communities and no survivor testimony. It only reads the company’s own policy documents, processes them in memory, and stores nothing after the report is produced.
